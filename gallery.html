Whale shark

Rhincodon typus

The largest fish in the sea, and completely harmless to people. A whale shark swims slowly near the surface with its mouth wide open, filtering plankton and small fish from the water. The pattern of white spots is unique to each animal, which lets researchers identify individuals from photos like this one.

Length
Up to ~18 m
Diet
Plankton
Range
Warm seas
IUCN status
Endangered

Photo: Tom Huiskes

Source: IUCN Red List (2016). Threats: collisions with ships, bycatch and fishing.
